The product

What is AviaFrame?

AviaFrame is a white-label flight booking platform for travel agencies in the Gulf and Middle East. Each agency gets their own branded website and booking widget that they embed on their existing site or share as a standalone link.

🌐

Branded agency site

A fully customised website deployed at agencyname.aviaframe.com with their logo, colors, and content.

✈️

Booking widget

An embeddable search & booking widget they can place on any website — their own domain, social link-in-bio, or anywhere.

💰

Commission on every ticket

They earn a configurable percentage on every ticket sold. They can adjust their rate anytime from the admin panel.

📊

Orders dashboard

Real-time view of all bookings — passenger details, status, ticket issuance — in one place.

Qualifying

Who is a good fit?

✓ Good fit

  • Existing travel agency with clients but no online booking
  • Agency with a website that wants to add a booking feature
  • New agency that needs a quick, professional web presence
  • Agency selling mostly domestic Gulf routes (Saudi, UAE, Kuwait, etc.)
  • Agency that needs Arabic-language support
  • Corporate travel desk wanting to brand a booking tool

✗ Poor fit

  • Pure retail customer (not a business/agency)
  • Agency only doing hotel or package tours (we're flights-only for now)
  • Agency that already has a full booking engine integrated
  • Agency with no internet presence and no plan to build one
The pitch

How to position AviaFrame

One sentence: "AviaFrame gives your agency a professional branded booking website and widget in 24 hours — you earn commission on every ticket, we handle the tech."

  1. Ready in 24 hours. We build and deploy the site — they don't touch a line of code.
  2. Their brand, not ours. Logo, colors, content, custom subdomain — fully white-label.

  4. Works on any website. The widget can be embedded on their existing site or used standalone.

Objection handling

Common objections

Objection Response
"We already have a website" The widget embeds on your existing site — it adds a booking engine to whatever you already have, no rebuild needed.
"How do we get paid?" Your commission is built into every ticket price. You set the percentage, customers pay the combined price, and we remit your earnings on agreed terms.
"What does it cost?" Speak to the team for current pricing. Revenue share means your costs are aligned with sales volume.
"We're not tech-savvy" You don't need to be. Your team only sees a simple dashboard with orders and commission. We manage all the technical setup.
"Is the inventory good?" We access live fares via a major GDS/aggregator. The same inventory large OTAs use.
"What if we want to change the site design later?" Contact us — we handle all branding updates. Your team focuses on sales, we maintain the platform.
The onboarding process

Step-by-step: from deal to live site

1

Deal is agreed → fill in the onboarding form

Open aviaframe.com/agency-onboard.html and fill in all required fields on behalf of the agency (or share the link for them to fill in). Collect logo, colors, about text, top destinations, and contact info. The more detail, the better the first site will look.

2

AviaFrame team receives the form → creates the agency

The super admin creates the agency in the system, uploads branding, sets up commission, and deploys the site. This typically takes 1–4 hours during business hours.

3

Agency receives a welcome email

Sent automatically to the admin email they provided. Contains login link, agency site URL, widget key, and quick-start instructions. See the email preview below.

4

Agency completes their own setup

They sign in to admin.aviaframe.com, set their commission rate, add their website domain to the widget domains list, copy the embed snippet, and paste it on their site. This takes about 15 minutes.

5

Test booking + go live

Run a test booking with a real card (or use sandbox mode) to confirm everything works end to end. Once confirmed — they're live and ready to sell.

6

Follow-up: branding updates

If the agency wants to update their site (new logo, hero photo, destinations) they contact the AviaFrame team. We update and redeploy — usually within the same business day.

Agency admin panel

What the agency sees in their portal

The agency admin at admin.aviaframe.com has a focused, simple view:

📋

Orders

All bookings made through their widget or site. Passenger names, flight details, payment status, ticket issuance status.

💸

Commission settings

Their current commission % — they can adjust it anytime. Change takes effect on the next booking immediately.

🔗

Widget domains

Domains where the booking widget is allowed to load. They add their website domain here for the widget to work.

📦

Widget embed code

Ready-to-copy HTML snippet they paste on their website to add the booking widget. Read-only — just copy & paste.

Important: Agency admins cannot change their site branding (logo, colors, hero, destinations). All site updates go through the AviaFrame team. This keeps Netlify bandwidth within plan limits and ensures quality control.

Quick reference

Pre-submission checklist

Before submitting the onboarding form, make sure you have:

  • Agency name in English (and Arabic if applicable)
  • Admin contact email — this becomes their login
  • Contact phone number
  • Agreed subdomain (e.g. agencyname.aviaframe.com)
  • Country
  • Agreed commission rate (%)
  • Logo URL (PNG/SVG, transparent background)
  • Primary brand color (hex code)
  • About us text in English (min 2 sentences)

Everything else (hero, destinations, social, working hours) can be added later. The site will deploy with defaults if some fields are blank.
