AviaFrame Agency Setup Guide

Your step-by-step guide to setting up your AviaFrame account: branding your site, configuring payments, publishing your public booking page, and running a final test before you go live.

Setup overview

Your agency account is already created. To go live, you need to complete branding, configure your payment settings, publish your public booking site, and run a quick end-to-end test.

1 Admin portal: admin.aviaframe.com
4 Steps to go live: brand → payments → publish → test
2 Outcomes: branded public booking site and embeddable widget
Where to start: Log in to admin.aviaframe.com with the email address this guide was sent to. All settings, branding, and publish controls are inside the portal.

Your setup steps

Follow these steps in order. Each one unlocks the next. The portal will show you a checklist of required items — all of them need to be complete before you can publish your public site.

1

Brand your agency

In the portal, open your agency settings and fill in:

  • Upload your logo (PNG or SVG recommended)
  • Set your primary brand color and accent color
  • Add an about text in English (and Arabic if your market needs it)
  • Add working hours and office information
  • Add a Google Maps embed URL if you have a physical office
  • Add social media links (Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp, etc.)
2

Configure payment settings

Select which payment methods your customers can use:

  • Online card — Visa, Mastercard, Mada (Moyasar powered)
  • Cash — customer pays in person; agent issues ticket manually
  • Invoice — requires bank name and IBAN (shown to customers in payment instructions)

If you plan to embed the booking widget on your own website (not just the agency site), add your domain to the Allowed widget domains list.

3

Review the setup checklist

The portal shows a setup checklist. Make sure the following are complete before you publish:

✓
Required before first publish
Agency name, subdomain, contact email, phone number, brand colors, and at least one payment method.
✓
Strongly recommended before go-live
Logo, about text in English, office hours, and a completed booking smoke test.

Publish and testing

Once your checklist is complete, publish your public booking site and run a quick end-to-end test to confirm everything works before sharing with customers.

4

Publish your public site

In the admin portal, click Publish site (or Republish if you are updating an already-live site).

  • Publishing takes 1–2 minutes.
  • After publish, your site will be live at your agency domain (e.g. youragency.aviaframe.com).
  • Any time you update branding, contacts, or payment settings, click Republish to push the changes live.
Not ready to go public yet? You can complete all settings and leave the site unpublished until you are ready. Customers cannot access the booking page until you click Publish.
5

Run a booking smoke test

After your site is live, do a quick end-to-end test:

  1. Open your public agency site.
  2. Search for a real flight route (e.g. RUH → DXB, any future date).
  3. Select an offer and fill in passenger details.
  4. Complete the payment using a real card (you can cancel the booking afterwards if needed).
  5. Check that you receive the booking confirmation email and ticket PDF.
If you embedded the widget on your own website: Test both the agency site and the embedded widget origin to confirm both work correctly.

Your access & credentials

Everything you need to operate your agency account is in the setup email you received. Here is a summary of what to keep handy.

1
Admin portal
admin.aviaframe.com — log in with the email address your setup invitation was sent to.
2
Your public agency site
After first publish, your booking site will be live at youragency.aviaframe.com. The exact URL is shown in the portal and in your setup email.
3
Widget API key
Your public API key (starts with ag_) is shown in the portal under your agency settings. You need it to embed the booking widget on your own website.
4
Need help?
Contact AviaFrame support at support@aviaframe.com and include your agency name and domain.
