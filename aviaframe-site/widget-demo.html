What agencies embed

A target container plus a secure loader script. The browser never receives supplier credentials or DRCT secrets.

How tenant scoping works

The public agency key identifies the tenant. The backend validates allowed domains, then issues a short-lived widget session tied to that origin.

Where to integrate

Use this demo to test search UX, then open the integration guide for the exact snippet, onboarding steps, and domain allowlist setup.

How it works: Search for a flight → select an offer → fill in passenger details → pay with your card via Moyasar. After successful payment, a PDF e-ticket is generated and sent to the email you enter. The order appears in admin.aviaframe.com with status Issued.

To embed on your website: use the secure loader with your public agency key. See Partner Integration for the exact snippet and onboarding steps.
